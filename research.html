Research & development

Research that starts with how tissue is actually used.

R&D priorities below are presented as a realistic development roadmap: observe the use case, define the target, test the change and document the outcome.

Use-case research

Research the moment, not just the material.

A tissue product can feel very different depending on the user, surface, amount of moisture and dispensing method. EcoWipe's development model can look at the full interaction rather than a single lab number.

Softnesssurface feel, facial comfort and perception
Absorbencyuptake, speed and capacity for the task
Strengthsheet integrity before and during use
Dispensingpack, fold, roll and dispenser interaction
01

Observe

Document how different users handle the sheet, pack or dispenser and where failures occur.

02

Prototype

Change one or more variables: fibre blend, GSM, ply, embossing, fold, lotion or pack design.

03

Validate

Compare the prototype against defined performance targets and user requirements before release.

Research areas

Useful questions for future development.

  • Can a softer sheet retain acceptable wet and dry strength?
  • Can absorbency improve without making the sheet feel bulky?
  • Can pack design reduce wasted material while protecting the product?
  • Can dispenser geometry improve one-at-a-time dispensing?
  • Which formats are most useful across home and institutional buyers?
